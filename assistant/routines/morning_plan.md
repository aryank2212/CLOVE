This is the user's morning brief. It runs unattended and is read-only: ask no questions, just write the brief as
your final message, short enough to read in under a minute.

Tone: calm, direct, no hype.

1. Read yesterday's entry from today's memory (the day log CLOVE keeps). If there is none, say plainly that
   yesterday wasn't logged. Never invent numbers or tasks.
2. Read the open to-dos.
3. Write the brief, in this order:
   - **Yesterday**: one line on what got done and the one thing the user wanted to fix.
   - **Today**: the three most important to-dos, most important first.
   - **First step**: the single smallest action that starts the most important one.

Edit this file to plan your mornings your way: point it at your own notes, calendar or planning page.
