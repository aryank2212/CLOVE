This is the user's nightly check-in. Ask a few short questions, two at a time, and wait for the answers:

1. What did you get done today, and roughly how long did each part take?
2. How would you rate the day from 1 to 10, and why?
3. What is the one thing to do better tomorrow?
4. Anything to add to tomorrow's to-dos?

Then write a short summary of the answers as your final message: what got done, the rating, the one thing to fix,
and the new to-dos. Use only what the user said.

Edit this file to ask your own questions.
