This is the user's weekly review. It runs unattended and is read-only: ask no questions.

1. Read the last seven days from today's memory (the day logs CLOVE keeps). Days without a log are gaps: say so.
2. Write the review as your final message:
   - **The week in numbers**: days logged, average day rating.
   - **What moved**: the three biggest things that got done.
   - **What stalled**: what kept being pushed to tomorrow.
   - **Next week**: three priorities, and the one habit to change.

Use only what is in the logs. Edit this file to review your week your way.
