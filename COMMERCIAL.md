# Commercial licence

CLOVE is available under two licences. Pick the one that fits.

## AGPL-3.0 (free)

Use, change and share CLOVE at no cost under the [GNU Affero General Public License v3.0](LICENSE). The main
condition: if you distribute CLOVE, a modified CLOVE, or a product that includes it, or let people use it over a
network, you must release the complete source code of that work under the AGPL too.

Good for: personal use, learning, research, and open-source projects.

## Commercial licence (paid)

A commercial licence lets you use CLOVE without the AGPL's conditions:

- ship CLOVE, or a product built on it, without publishing your source code;
- offer it as a hosted or managed service;
- rebrand it as your own assistant (your name, voice, icon and colours);
- get help setting it up, and fixes for the platforms you need.

Terms and price depend on how you'll use it (a single company, a product you sell, a service). To ask, open an
issue titled **"Commercial licence"** on this repository, or contact
[@aryank2212](https://github.com/aryank2212) on GitHub.

## What a commercial licence does not cover

- **Clove Notch** (`notch/`) is MIT-licensed already, from Coucou; keep its copyright notice in what you ship.
- **Third-party services**: Google Gemini, Anthropic Claude and other providers have their own terms, and you
  need your own keys and accounts for them.
- **Third-party components** listed in [NOTICE.md](NOTICE.md) keep their own licences.
